# Pakistan Solar Pricing

> **Prices are approximate planning references, not quotations. Prices change with exchange rates, imports, taxes, supplier margin, availability, city and installation scope. Checked: August 2026.**

|---|---:|
| 3 kW | PKR 350,000–600,000 |
| 5 kW | PKR 500,000–1,000,000 |

| 15 kW | PKR 1,300,000–3,000,000 |

Published 2026 sources differ considerably. For example, one July 2026 market guide reported 3 kW at PKR 370k–594k, 5 kW at PKR 617k–990k, 10 kW at PKR 1.235m–1.98m and 15 kW at PKR 1.852m–2.97m. Another source reported lower ranges. Treat these as market references only.

## Panel Price Per Watt
Recent 2026 market references put many Tier-1 modules roughly around **PKR 33–45/W**, depending on model and supplier. One July 2026 listing reported a 615 W panel at PKR 25,215, about PKR 41/W.

## Inverter Price
A broad 2026 planning reference reported Tier-1 inverter pricing around **PKR 32,000–55,000 per kW**, but exact prices vary strongly by model, phase, hybrid capability, battery compatibility and brand.

## Battery Price Per kWh
Do not compare batteries on nominal PKR/kWh alone. Compare usable kWh, chemistry, cycle life, power rating, warranty and compatible inverter. LiFePO4 normally costs more upfront than lead-acid but may provide higher usable energy and longer cycle life.

## Hybrid Systems
Adding battery storage substantially increases system cost. The chatbot should ask for required backup hours, critical loads, battery chemistry, battery capacity and inverter model before estimating a hybrid system.

## Financing
Compare cash price, down payment, financed amount, markup/profit/interest, fees, tenure, monthly payment, total repayment and early-settlement terms. Do not quote a current bank financing offer without checking the bank's official source.

## Net Billing
Do not hard-code a permanent export rate. NEPRA's Legal page lists the Prosumer Regulations 2026 and subsequent notifications/amendments. Verify the latest NEPRA and relevant DISCO rules before a current financial calculation.

## Chatbot Price Warning
**Initial estimate only. Solar prices in Pakistan change frequently. Actual cost depends on current equipment prices, brand/model, city, installation requirements, taxes and supplier quotation. Verify the latest quotation before purchase.**
