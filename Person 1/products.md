## Inverters

### Growatt
Growatt offers residential PV and storage inverters. Its MIN XH family includes models with battery interfaces and monitoring options. Exact MPPT, voltage, current and power limits are model-specific.

### Huawei
Huawei Smart PV includes SUN2000 inverter families. Exact models have different MPPT ranges, input limits, output power and battery compatibility.

### Sungrow
Sungrow offers residential and commercial grid-connected and hybrid inverter families. Selection depends on PV input, AC output, MPPT configuration, battery compatibility and local grid requirements.

### Inverex
Inverex is a Pakistan-market solar brand offering panels, on-grid/hybrid inverters and batteries. Check current Pakistan listings for model availability and price.

### GoodWe
GoodWe offers on-grid, hybrid and storage inverter families. Battery compatibility, MPPT range, power and backup capability depend on model.

## Batteries

## LiFePO4 vs Lead-Acid

| Feature | LiFePO4 | Lead-Acid |
|---|---|---|
| Usable energy | Generally higher | Generally lower |
| Cycle life | Generally higher | Generally lower |
| Weight | Lower | Higher |
| Upfront cost | Usually higher | Usually lower |
| Maintenance | Usually lower | Can be higher |
| Typical fit | Frequent cycling | Budget/specific applications |

This is a general comparison; actual results depend on model and operating conditions.

## Recommendation Examples
**Residential on-grid:** compare a suitable Tier-1 panel with a correctly sized on-grid inverter. **Initial estimate only.**

**Home needing backup:** compare a hybrid inverter and compatible battery instead of assuming a normal grid-tied inverter provides backup.

**Frequent cycling:** compare LiFePO4 and lead-acid using usable kWh, cycle life, warranty, power and total cost.
