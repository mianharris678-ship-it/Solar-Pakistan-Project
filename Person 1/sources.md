1. Current Pakistan law/regulation or NEPRA notification
2. Current DISCO/utility notification
3. Current official manufacturer datasheet/manual
4. Current official/local product listing
5. Reputable current Pakistan market source
6. Project research document
7. General educational source

## Accuracy Rules

* Do not present an old price as a guaranteed current price.
* Do not hard-code a permanent export/net-billing rate.
* Verify current NEPRA/DISCO rules for regulatory questions.
* Use exact manufacturer model datasheets for technical specifications.
* Label customer sizing/pricing as an **initial estimate** unless professionally assessed.
* If sources disagree, report a range and explain that model, location, supplier and market conditions can cause variation.
