# Solar Basics

## Solar Panels
Solar photovoltaic (PV) panels convert sunlight into DC electricity. Panel selection depends on rated power, efficiency, electrical characteristics, dimensions, warranty, roof area, shading and price. Modern modules commonly use monocrystalline, N-type/TOPCon and bifacial technologies.

## Inverter
An inverter converts DC electricity into AC electricity. It may also provide MPPT, monitoring and protection.
- On-grid: utility-connected.
- Off-grid: independent operation, normally with batteries.
- Hybrid: coordinates solar, battery and grid power.

## Battery
Batteries store energy for later use. Common technologies are lead-acid and lithium/LiFePO4. Compare usable kWh, DoD, charge/discharge power, cycle life, warranty and inverter compatibility.

## kW vs kWh
**kW** measures power. **kWh** measures energy. A 2 kW load operating for 3 hours uses about 6 kWh.

## Peak Sun Hours
The project research uses approximate planning values: Islamabad 5.5, Lahore 5.0, Karachi 5.8, Peshawar 5.7 and Quetta 6.0 PSH/day. These are assumptions, not guarantees. Weather, season, temperature, dust, shading and system losses affect actual generation.

## System Types
**On-grid:** connected to the utility grid and normally without a large battery.
**Off-grid:** independent of the utility grid and normally requires batteries.
**Hybrid:** combines PV, batteries and grid power and can provide backup to selected loads.
