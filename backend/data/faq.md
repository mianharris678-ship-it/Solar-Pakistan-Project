# Solar FAQ

## 1. What is solar energy?
Solar energy comes from sunlight. PV panels convert sunlight into DC electricity, while an inverter can convert it to AC for compatible loads. Pakistan can use on-grid, off-grid and hybrid PV systems. **Source:** Project source; manufacturer documentation. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 2. How do solar panels work?
PV cells convert sunlight into DC electrical energy. Multiple cells form modules and modules form an array. Actual output depends on sunlight, temperature, shading, orientation and losses. **Source:** Project source; manufacturer documentation. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 3. What is the difference between kW and kWh?
kW measures power, while kWh measures energy over time. A 2 kW load running for 3 hours consumes about 6 kWh. Solar system capacity is commonly stated in kW and electricity consumption in kWh. **Source:** Project source. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 4. What is a solar inverter?
An inverter converts DC from PV panels into AC. Depending on the model it can also provide MPPT, monitoring, protection and battery management. Selection depends on PV capacity, load, phase, voltage/current limits and battery needs. **Source:** Growatt/Huawei/Sungrow/GoodWe documentation. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 5. What is MPPT?
MPPT means Maximum Power Point Tracking. It helps an inverter operate a PV array near its maximum available power point as sunlight and temperature change. Exact MPPT voltage/current limits are model-specific. **Source:** Manufacturer datasheets. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 6. What is an on-grid system?
An on-grid system is connected to the utility. It normally does not need a large battery. A standard grid-tied inverter generally shuts down during an outage unless suitable backup/islanding equipment is installed. **Source:** Manufacturer documentation; project source. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 7. What is an off-grid system?
An off-grid system operates independently of the utility grid and normally requires batteries. Battery sizing depends on critical load, backup duration and available solar generation. **Source:** Project source; battery/inverter documentation. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 8. What is a hybrid system?
A hybrid system combines solar, battery storage and grid power. It can use solar during the day, charge batteries and provide backup to selected loads when correctly configured. **Source:** Manufacturer documentation. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 9. Do I need batteries?
No. Many on-grid systems operate without a large battery. Batteries are mainly useful for backup, self-consumption and off-grid operation. The decision depends on outage frequency, critical loads and budget. **Source:** Project source. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 10. What is LiFePO4?
LiFePO4 means lithium iron phosphate. It is a lithium battery chemistry widely used for energy storage and generally offers high usable energy and cycle life. Exact performance depends on model, temperature, DoD and warranty. **Source:** BYD/Pylontech documentation. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 11. LiFePO4 vs lead-acid?
LiFePO4 generally has higher usable energy, longer cycle life and lower weight, but higher upfront cost. Lead-acid can have a lower initial cost. Compare usable kWh, cycle life, warranty, power and total cost rather than purchase price alone. **Source:** Manufacturer documentation. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 12. How many panels do I need?
Divide required PV watts by panel watts for a preliminary count. For 5,000 W using 550 W panels, 5,000/550 = 9.09, so simple rounding gives 10 panels. **Initial estimate only:** final string sizing must check inverter limits and site conditions. **Source:** Project calculation framework. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 13. How much can a 5 kW system generate?
A preliminary calculation can use PV capacity × local PSH × assumed efficiency. The project uses about 5.0 PSH/day for Lahore and 75–80% efficiency for planning. Actual output changes with season, weather, temperature, dust and shading. **Initial estimate only. Source:** Project source. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 14. Does shading reduce solar production?
Yes. Shading can significantly reduce array output, particularly in string-connected systems. Trees, walls and buildings should be checked during relevant solar hours. **Initial estimate only:** a site survey is required for reliable assessment. **Source:** Project source; manufacturer guidance. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 15. Do panels work on cloudy days?
Yes, PV can produce electricity from diffuse sunlight, but output is normally lower than under strong direct sunlight. Daily generation can therefore vary considerably with weather and season. **Source:** Project source. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 16. Which panel brand is best: LONGi, Jinko, Trina, Canadian Solar or JA Solar?
There is no universal best brand. Compare the exact model's power, efficiency, electrical characteristics, warranty, dimensions, availability and price. **Initial recommendation only:** use current manufacturer datasheets and local supplier information. **Source:** Manufacturer sources. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 17. Which inverter is better: Growatt, Huawei, Sungrow, Inverex or GoodWe?
The best choice depends on system size, phase, PV input, MPPT requirements, battery compatibility, backup needs, warranty and service. Compare exact models rather than brand names alone. **Initial recommendation only:** verify the current model datasheet and local service. **Source:** Manufacturer sources; Inverex Pakistan. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 18. What are Maxpower, Pylontech and BYD batteries?
They are battery products/brands used for energy storage. Pylontech and BYD publish model-specific lithium storage documentation; Maxpower products vary by model. Always verify chemistry, usable capacity, voltage, compatibility and warranty for the exact model. **Source:** Manufacturer/product sources. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 19. What is net metering in Pakistan?
Net metering historically described a grid-connected arrangement where exported electricity received a regulatory credit. Pakistan's framework has changed, so an old net-metering rate should not be assumed current. **Source:** NEPRA Legal page. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 20. What is net billing in Pakistan?
Pakistan's current framework includes the NEPRA Prosumer Regulations 2026. Treatment of imported/exported electricity depends on the applicable regulations, agreements and utility procedures. **Safe answer:** verify the latest NEPRA notification/amendment and relevant DISCO rules before applying or calculating revenue. **Source:** NEPRA. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 21. What does NEPRA do?
NEPRA is Pakistan's National Electric Power Regulatory Authority. It publishes regulations, decisions, licences and related electricity-sector material. For solar interconnection questions, the latest NEPRA documents and relevant utility procedures should be used. **Source:** NEPRA official website. **Checked:** August 2026.

## 22. Will solar eliminate my electricity bill?
Not necessarily. Remaining charges can depend on imported electricity, fixed charges, taxes, adjustments, consumption pattern and current export treatment. **Initial estimate only:** use actual bills and current tariff/regulatory information. **Source:** NEPRA; project pricing framework. **Checked:** August 2026.
